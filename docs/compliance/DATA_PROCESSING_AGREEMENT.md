## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1** "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person as defined in applicable Data Protection Laws.

**1.2** "Data Protection Laws" means all applicable laws relating to data protection and privacy, including but not limited to GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable regulations.

**1.3** "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, or deletion.

**1.4** "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

**1.5** "Data Subject" means an identified or identifiable natural person whose Personal Data is processed.

**1.6** "Services" means the Skopaq E2E Testing Platform and related services provided under the Master Service Agreement.

---

## 2. SCOPE AND PURPOSE

**2.1** This DPA applies to the Processing of Personal Data by Skopaq on behalf of Customer in connection with the provision of the Services.

**2.2** The nature and purpose of Processing, categories of Data Subjects, and types of Personal Data are described in **Schedule A**.

**2.3** Skopaq shall process Personal Data only:
- (a) In accordance with Customer's documented instructions;
- (b) As necessary to provide the Services;
- (c) As required by applicable law.

---

## 3. DATA PROCESSING OBLIGATIONS

### 3.1 Processor Obligations

Skopaq agrees to:

**(a)** Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer, unless required by applicable law;

**(b)** Ensure that persons authorized to process Personal Data have committed to confidentiality;

**(c)** Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures as described in **Schedule B**;

**(d)** Assist Customer in responding to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights;

**(e)** Assist Customer in ensuring compliance with security, breach notification, and impact assessment obligations;

**(f)** At Customer's choice, delete or return all Personal Data upon termination of Services;

**(g)** Make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA;

**(h)** Allow for and contribute to audits conducted by Customer or an auditor mandated by Customer.

### 3.2 Controller Obligations

Customer agrees to:

**(a)** Ensure lawful basis for Processing and transparency to Data Subjects;

**(b)** Provide documented instructions for Processing;

**(c)** Ensure accuracy of Personal Data provided to Skopaq;

**(d)** Notify Skopaq promptly of any changes affecting Processing.

---

## 4. SUB-PROCESSING

### 4.1 Authorized Sub-processors

Customer authorizes Skopaq to engage the Sub-processors listed in **Schedule C** for the purposes described therein.

### 4.2 Sub-processor Requirements

Skopaq shall:

**(a)** Enter into written agreements with Sub-processors imposing data protection obligations no less protective than this DPA;

**(b)** Remain liable for Sub-processor compliance;

**(c)** Notify Customer of any intended changes to Sub-processors at least 30 days in advance;

**(d)** Provide Customer the opportunity to object to Sub-processor changes.

### 4.3 Customer Objection

If Customer objects to a new Sub-processor on reasonable grounds related to data protection, the parties shall discuss the objection in good faith. If no resolution is reached, Customer may terminate the affected Services without penalty.

---

## 5. DATA TRANSFERS

### 5.1 Transfer Mechanisms

Any transfer of Personal Data outside the European Economic Area (EEA) shall be conducted in accordance with:

**(a)** Standard Contractual Clauses (EU Commission Decision 2021/914) incorporated as **Schedule D**;

**(b)** Adequacy decisions by the European Commission;

**(c)** Other lawful transfer mechanisms under GDPR.

### 5.2 Self-Hosted Deployments

For self-hosted deployments where Customer controls data storage location, Customer is responsible for ensuring appropriate transfer mechanisms are in place for any data transfers.

### 5.3 Air-Gap Deployments

For air-gap deployments, no Personal Data is transferred outside Customer's infrastructure, and this Section 5 does not apply.

---

## 6. SECURITY MEASURES

### 6.1 Technical and Organizational Measures

Skopaq implements the security measures described in **Schedule B**, including:

**(a)** Encryption of Personal Data at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3);

**(b)** Access controls and authentication (multi-factor authentication, role-based access);

**(c)** Regular security testing and vulnerability assessments;

**(d)** Incident detection and response procedures;

**(e)** Business continuity and disaster recovery measures.

### 6.2 Security Updates

Skopaq may update security measures provided that updates do not materially decrease the overall security of the Services.

---

## 7. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

### 7.1 Notification Timeline

Skopaq shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data breach.

### 7.2 Notification Content

Notification shall include:

**(a)** Description of the nature of the breach;

**(b)** Categories and approximate number of Data Subjects affected;

**(c)** Categories and approximate number of records affected;

**(d)** Contact point for further information;

**(e)** Likely consequences of the breach;

**(f)** Measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

### 7.3 Cooperation

Skopaq shall cooperate with Customer and provide reasonable assistance in investigating and mitigating the breach.

---

## 8. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

### 8.1 Assistance with Requests

Skopaq shall assist Customer in responding to Data Subject requests regarding:

**(a)** Access to Personal Data;

**(b)** Rectification of inaccurate data;

**(c)** Erasure of data ("right to be forgotten");

**(d)** Restriction of processing;

**(e)** Data portability;

**(f)** Objection to processing.

### 8.2 Response Timeline

Skopaq shall respond to Customer requests for assistance within 10 business days.

### 8.3 Direct Requests

If Skopaq receives a request directly from a Data Subject, Skopaq shall promptly redirect the request to Customer unless legally prohibited from doing so.

---

## 9. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

### 9.1 Retention Period

Skopaq shall retain Personal Data only for the duration necessary to provide the Services or as required by applicable law.

### 9.2 Deletion Upon Termination

Upon termination of Services, Skopaq shall, at Customer's election:

**(a)** Delete all Personal Data within 30 days; or

**(b)** Return all Personal Data in a commonly used format.

### 9.3 Certification

Upon request, Skopaq shall certify in writing that all Personal Data has been deleted in accordance with this Section 9.

### 9.4 Exceptions

Skopaq may retain Personal Data where required by applicable law, provided that:

**(a)** Skopaq notifies Customer of such requirement;

**(b)** Retention is limited to the extent and duration required by law;

**(c)** Skopaq maintains confidentiality of retained data.

---

## 10. AUDITS

### 10.1 Audit Rights

Customer may audit Skopaq's compliance with this DPA:

**(a)** Upon 30 days' written notice;

**(b)** Not more than once per year (unless required following a breach);

**(c)** During normal business hours;

**(d)** Subject to reasonable confidentiality requirements.

### 10.2 Third-Party Audits

Skopaq shall make available SOC 2 Type II reports and other relevant certifications upon request.

### 10.3 Audit Costs

Customer shall bear its own costs of conducting audits. Skopaq shall bear costs of providing audit access and documentation.

---

## 11. LIABILITY

### 11.1 Allocation

Each party shall be liable for damages arising from its breach of this DPA and applicable Data Protection Laws.

### 11.2 Limitation

Liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations set forth in the Master Service Agreement, except that such limitations shall not apply to:

**(a)** Violations of Data Protection Laws due to willful misconduct;

**(b)** Unauthorized disclosure of Personal Data.

---

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 12.1 Term

This DPA shall remain in effect for the duration of the Master Service Agreement.

### 12.2 Survival

Sections 6 (Security), 7 (Breach Notification), 9 (Deletion), 10 (Audits), and 11 (Liability) shall survive termination.

---

## 13. GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 13.1 Governing Law

This DPA shall be governed by the laws of [Jurisdiction], without regard to conflict of law principles.

### 13.2 Amendments

This DPA may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

### 13.3 Entire Agreement

This DPA, together with its Schedules and the Master Service Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement regarding data protection.

### 13.4 Severability

If any provision is found invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.

# SCHEDULE A: PROCESSING DETAILS

## A.1 Nature and Purpose of Processing

| Purpose | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Test Execution | Execute automated UI and API tests against Customer applications |
| Test Result Storage | Store test results, screenshots, and recordings |
| Self-Healing | Analyze test failures and suggest/apply fixes |
| Reporting | Generate test reports and analytics |
| User Management | Manage Customer user accounts and permissions |

## A.2 Categories of Data Subjects

- Customer employees using the Skopaq platform
- End users of Customer applications (when captured in test recordings)

## A.3 Types of Personal Data

| Data Category | Examples | Retention |
|---------------|----------|-----------|
| User Identity | Name, email, profile picture | Account lifetime |
| Authentication | Hashed passwords, API keys | Account lifetime |
| Usage Data | Login timestamps, actions performed | 90 days |
| Test Artifacts | Screenshots containing PII (if any) | Configurable (default 30 days) |
| Audit Logs | User actions, IP addresses | 1 year |

## A.4 Special Categories of Data

Skopaq does not intentionally process special categories of data (health, biometric, etc.). Customer is responsible for ensuring test scenarios do not capture such data.

## A.5 Processing Duration

Processing shall continue for the duration of the Services Agreement plus the retention periods specified above.
